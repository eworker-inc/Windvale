# Decision 0028: MIT license and E-Worker stewardship

- Date: 2026-07-30
- Status: Accepted and implemented; copyright-notice scope clarified by [Decision 0031](0031-AI-Authorship-And-Vendor-Neutrality.md) and public contribution policy completed by [Decision 0032](0032-Public-Contribution-And-Governance-Foundation.md)

## Context

Windvale was created with open-source intent, but an intent statement is not a license. Public use, modification, redistribution, commercial adoption, and contribution all require an explicit grant. The project also needs an identified business steward without implying that contributors receive warranties or that every future governance question is already settled.

## Decision

Release Windvale under the MIT License with copyright held by E-Worker Inc. E-Worker Inc is the project business and steward.

The root `LICENSE` file is authoritative for repository content unless a file or incorporated third-party component carries a compatible, separately identified notice. Copies or substantial portions must retain the copyright and permission notice. The license permits private, public, and commercial use, modification, distribution, sublicensing, and sale without a copyleft requirement, and it supplies the standard MIT warranty and liability disclaimer.

Repository contributions are intended for distribution under the same MIT terms. A separate contributor license agreement is not introduced by this decision; contribution, provenance, security-reporting, trademark, and governance policies remain later public-foundation work.

## Consequences

Windvale can now be accurately described as open source rather than merely open-source-intended. Individuals, research groups, and businesses can use the project without seeking a separate commercial license, while attribution and the disclaimer travel with substantial copies.

MIT licensing does not make aspirational compiler, native, or operating-system milestones complete, certify fitness for a purpose, remove third-party license obligations, or transfer Windvale/E-Worker names and marks. Public positioning must continue to distinguish implemented and qualified evidence from future scope.

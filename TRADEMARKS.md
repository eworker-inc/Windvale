E-Worker Inc uses the names **Windvale** and **E-Worker**, and associated project logos or visual identity, to identify the official project and its stewardship. This policy does not state that any name or mark is registered in every jurisdiction.

Without separate permission, you may:

- Refer truthfully to Windvale or E-Worker in commentary, compatibility statements, research, reviews, and attribution.
- State that software is based on or interoperates with Windvale.
- Link to the official project.
- Redistribute unmodified official releases while preserving their notices.
- Create a fork if its name and presentation clearly distinguish it from the official project.

Separate permission is required to:

- Present a modified release or service as official Windvale.
- Use project logos as the primary identity of another project, product, domain, package, event, or organization.
- Imply sponsorship, certification, partnership, or endorsement by E-Worker Inc.
- Register confusingly similar names or visual identities for a competing distribution or service.

When naming compatibility, prefer factual wording such as “compatible with Windvale WVB 1.6” and identify the exact supported contract. Compatibility language must not imply that E-Worker Inc tested or approved the implementation.

Questions about uses outside this policy should be directed to [info@eworker.ca](mailto:info@eworker.ca).

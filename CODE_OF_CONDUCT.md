# Windvale code of conduct

## Commitment

Windvale participants commit to a technically rigorous, welcoming, and harassment-free environment. Participation is open regardless of experience, identity, background, disability, nationality, appearance, religion, or other personal characteristic.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Give specific, actionable technical feedback.
- State uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, authorship policy, and licensing boundaries.
- Avoid pressuring others to disclose personal identities, private prompts, credentials, or unpublished work.
- Accept reasonable maintainer direction on scope, safety, and repository process.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Personal insults, deliberate humiliation, sustained disruption, or bad-faith misrepresentation.
- Publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith conduct or security concern.
- Using project channels for spam, fraud, malware distribution, credential collection, or unlawful activity.

Technical disagreement, rejection of a contribution, enforcement of scope, and requests for evidence are not misconduct when expressed professionally and applied in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies in official Windvale repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions, events, and other spaces represented as official project activity. It also applies when someone publicly represents the project or acts with delegated project authority.

## Reporting

Do not place sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. Use a private business contact route published at [eworker.ca](https://eworker.ca/) and identify the message as a Windvale conduct report. Provide only the information needed to understand the incident, including links or screenshots when safe.

E-Worker Inc will handle reports as privately as reasonably possible, subject to safety and legal obligations. Anyone involved in a report must recuse themselves from deciding its outcome. Knowingly false reports or retaliation may themselves violate this policy, but an unsubstantiated good-faith report does not.

## Enforcement

Depending on context and severity, maintainers may clarify expectations, edit or remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation, temporarily suspend access, permanently remove access, or refer credible threats and unlawful conduct to an appropriate service provider or authority.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and communicated to the affected participant when safe. The steward may publish an anonymized explanation when transparency materially benefits the community.

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) and other established open-source community practices.

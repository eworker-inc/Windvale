Earlier portrait August 2026

Confusing ourselves into a computing stack.

An early project portrait embraces the uncertainty: language, compiler layers, runtime, effects, concurrency, platforms, observability, and a great many arrows—all progressing beautifully.

Read the illustrated transcript

The headline reads, “To confuse your competition, you must first confuse yourself.” A crowded Windvale whiteboard connects source, frontend, middle-end, backend, type system, IR layers, effects, memory model, concurrency, runtime, observability, platforms, and distributed systems.

Onlookers ask, “What is that thing?”, “Is it a language?”, and “I don't even know anymore.” The builders answer, “I have a feeling this is revolutionary. (Or insane.)” and “I understand every word, and nothing at the same time.” The closing line says Windvale is progressing beautifully while still figuring out exactly what it has created.
